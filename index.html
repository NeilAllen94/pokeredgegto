FAQ

Common questions.

How accurate is the GTO guidance?
Preflop guidance is based on established GTO range data — the same ranges used in professional play. Postflop guidance comes from our CFR-trained solver built across common board textures, giving directionally correct GTO tendencies for each hand type: when to bet big with strong hands, when to protect by checking, when to mix in bluffs. It is a study and practice aid, not a play guarantee — poker always involves variance. Use it to build sharper intuition, not to replace thinking.
What does Pro unlock?
Pro removes the daily practice limit and opens the full postflop experience in the Live Advisor: real-time Win%, board texture analysis, street-by-street GTO guidance, opponent profiling, session stats and street plan coaching on every hand.
Does it cover tournaments?
Yes. The Trainer has a full tournament mode with escalating blind levels, bubble pressure and final table dynamics. Push/fold ranges and ICM-aware concepts are covered in the Study Guide and drill modes.
What's the difference between the Live Advisor and the Practice Trainer?
The Live Advisor is for guidance on real hands as they happen — enter your cards and board, get instant recommendations. The Practice Trainer is for structured improvement: play against an AI lineup, get every decision scored, and track your stats and leaks over time. Both work together to build a sharper, more consistent game.
Is it suitable for beginners?
Yes. The Study Hub covers fundamentals from the ground up — hand rankings, pot odds, preflop ranges, postflop concepts and GTO basics. The Practice Trainer explains every scoring decision, so you understand not just what the right play is but why. Experienced players will find value in the GTO guidance and stat tracking for plugging leaks.
How do I cancel Pro?
Cancel anytime in App Store Settings → Subscriptions → Poker Edge. Pro features stay active until the end of your current billing period, with no charges after.
