FAQ

Common questions.

Is it really free?
Yes. Preflop advice is free forever — no account, no sign-up. You also get 10 free postflop hands (lifetime) in the Live Advisor and 10 free practice hands per day in the Trainer. The Study Hub — guides, quiz, glossary — is always free with no limit.
What exactly does Pro unlock?
Pro removes the daily practice limit and opens the full postflop experience in the Live Advisor: real-time Win%, board texture analysis, street-by-street GTO advice, opponent profiling and street plan coaching on every hand.
How accurate is the advice?
Advice is based on GTO (game theory optimal) principles, Chen formula hand scoring and established preflop range data. It's a study and practice aid, not a play-guarantee — poker always involves variance. Use it to build sharper intuition, not to replace thinking.
Does it cover tournaments?
Yes. The Trainer has a full tournament mode with escalating blind levels, bubble pressure and final table dynamics. Push/fold ranges and ICM-aware concepts are covered in the Study Guide and drill modes.
Can I use it at the table?
The Live Advisor is designed to be fast — minimal taps to get advice. Check your venue's rules before using any assistance tool in a live game; home games and online sites will vary.
How do I cancel Pro?
Cancel anytime in App Store Settings → Subscriptions → Poker Edge. Pro features stay active until the end of your current billing period, with no charges after.
